**THE BLOWUP MECHANISM (blowup_mode.py probe, 2026-08-27):** the +y collapse is a **pure formation SHEAR** — the drones RIP APART (relative velocity explodes to ~1e6 m/s) while the **center of mass barely moves** (||v_mean|| ~0.3 the whole time) and **the LOAD stays regulated** (~0.3 m off through the whole event). So it's LOAD-INVISIBLE: any load-referenced metric (coord2=||sum f_int||^2, leak2=||G@f_int||^2) is BLIND to it — the tearing force is in the NULLSPACE of G (G@F_null~0 by construction). Structure over time: healthy baseline shear ~1.0-1.3; RECURRENT survivable EXCURSIONS to v~4-15 for ~10 s (they mostly recover, envelope grows); then a ~0.2 s final CLIFF (80->1e6). So it's "usually fine, suddenly explodes" for any single excursion, but there ARE many dense excursions ~5-10 s before blowup to learn from.

**PRECURSOR 1 — OVERSPEED (the DIRECT blowup guardrail, drone-side speed):** since v_mean~=0, per-drone speed ~= the shear -> penalizing drone speed IS the shear penalty (load-invisible mode). Env: `overspeed_v=3.0` (was 4; healthy peaks ~2.5-3, pathological >4 -> line at 3), `overspeed_w=1.0` (was 0.2), `overspeed_cap=75.0` (was 30), `overspeed_lin=2.0` (NEW linear hinge). Penalty per drone = `overspeed_w * min(over^2 + overspeed_lin*over, cap)`, over=max(0,v-3). Zero in normal ops (can't disturb tracking), bites the excursions. v=3.5->1.25, 4->3, 6->15, capped 75. lin term gives immediate bite at the line (pure quadratic starts at zero slope).

**PRECURSOR 2 — OVERSHOOT hinge (INDIRECT, UN-SATURATE dlam):** env `overshoot_lam_w=1.0, overshoot_lam_lin=1.0, overshoot_lam_cap=9.0` (all NEW). Penalty = `w*min(over_sat^2 + lin*over_sat, cap)`, over_sat=max(0, sat_lam-1), sat_lam=||raw dlam||/cap_lam. **ZERO for sat<=1 (full cap authority is FREE); only bites the OVERSHOOT beyond cap — which is CLIPPED away anyway (applied dlam=cap whether raw is 1.0 or 1.5), so it costs the policy NOTHING REAL.** Equilibrium sat~=1 (max applied authority, no waste) -> does NOT starve authority (I wrongly feared it did). Discourages the whack-a-mole deep saturation that precedes the shear; aligns with oracle (dlam*~=0). WATCH via `satL`/`satW` now in the DET print (jerk dropped). CAUTION seen on the full-dist run: un-saturation (satL 1.14->0.83) IMPROVED DET_R but loop FLAT (saturation wasn't the binding constraint) and blowups did NOT fall (+y truncation is the real wall) — DET_R is NOT comparable across the reward change (added penalty terms).
