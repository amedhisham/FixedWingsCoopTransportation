**SCALE-GENERALIZATION HAS (AT LEAST) TWO INDEPENDENT FAILURE MODES — speed AND duration/displacement — NOT a single clean axis (user-tested, 2026-09-13).** For a quintic rest-to-rest move of displacement D over duration T: **v_peak = 1.875·D/T**, **a_peak = 5.77·D/T²**. Baseline regime: MIX_SCALE=5m, MIX_RAMP=16s -> v_peak ≈ 0.59 m/s. Four data points:
- **Baseline** (5m,16s): works.
- **Case A** (D×2, T×2 -> v_peak ×1.0, a_peak ×0.5): MILD degradation.
- **Case B** (D×2, T×1.5 -> v_peak ×1.33, a_peak ×0.89): BREAKS more.
- **Case C** (D×4, T×4 -> v_peak ×1.0, a_peak ×0.25): BREAKS.

**(CORRECTION of an earlier over-call "speed is THE binding axis" — Case C falsified it.)** C has the SAME peak speed as baseline (×1.0) and the LOWEST accel (×0.25) yet breaks -> speed alone is NOT it, and accel/jerk are ruled out (C is gentlest on both, worst outcome). No single kinematic scalar orders the four. The honest read: the residual is a LOCAL correction around (5m,16s); you fall off its manifold TWO ways:
1. **SPEED** (B vs A, matched at D=10m): higher v_peak -> off-distribution airspeed + bigger steady load-swing/lag angle (pendulum tilts back at speed; speed-dependent equilibrium, P14/P15 "static equilibria of a cable-suspended load with non-stop flying carriers") + obs-norm off-band.
2. **DURATION/DISPLACEMENT** (C vs A, matched at v=0.59): at constant speed a ×4 move takes 4x longer & goes 4x farther and breaks where ×2 only mildly degraded. Lines up with the KNOWN drift instability: the coordination leak's F_null SHEAR is load-invisible and INTEGRATES over time (recurrent ~10s excursions then a cliff, [[f2-wd-nullleak-breakthrough]] [[f2-shear-precursor-rewards]]) -> a longer episode gives the slow drift more time to reach the cliff. D and T are LOCKED (D∝T) at constant speed so this family can't separate displacement from duration.
